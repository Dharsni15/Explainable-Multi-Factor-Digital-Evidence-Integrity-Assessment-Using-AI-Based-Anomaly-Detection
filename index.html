Forensic Methodology & Architecture Justification
  • Why Isolation Forest? Real-world digital evidence forensics often lacks pre-labeled fraud datasets. Isolation Forest excels in detecting anomalous isolation paths across high-dimensional feature combinations without requiring prior supervision.
  • Why Multi-Factor? Single factors (such as hashes alone) only detect bit changes. Correlating metadata, timestamps, and custody links captures contextual tampering.
  • Why SHAP? Forensic examiners require transparent accountability to present in investigative proceedings explaining exactly which indicators caused a risk flag.
